Our Story

Where Every
Meal Feels
Like Home

Nestled at 1 Pademba Road, Freetown, Cokie's Cookery & Bar has become the beloved gathering place where authentic Sierra Leonean flavours meet warm hospitality. From freshly caught seafood grilled to perfection to our legendary spicy couscous and chicken, every dish tells the story of our rich culinary heritage.

The Experience

Taste, Sip, Savour

Three pillars define the Cokie's experience — each crafted with care, served with soul.

01

Home-Cooked Cuisine

Authentic Sierra Leonean recipes prepared with locally sourced ingredients. From cassava leaves to hearty meat dishes, every plate is a celebration of tradition.

02

BBQ & Grills

Freshly caught seafood and prime cuts, flame-kissed over open coals. Our grill masters turn every barbecue into an unforgettable experience.

03

Cocktails & Bar

From handcrafted cocktails to refreshing mocktails and smoothies, our bar caters to every palate. Don't miss our happy hour specials.
